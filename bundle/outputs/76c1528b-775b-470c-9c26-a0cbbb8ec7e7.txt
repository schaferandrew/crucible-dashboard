**1. Savings account**

\[
P = \$5\,000,\qquad r_{\text{annual}}=4\% ,\qquad n = 3 \text{ yr}
\]

Monthly rate  
\[
r_{\text{m}}=\frac{0.04}{12}=\frac1{300}=0.00333333\ldots
\]

Number of compounding periods  
\[
N = 3\times12=36
\]

Future value  
\[
FV=P(1+r_{\text{m}})^{N}=5000\left(1+\frac1{300}\right)^{36}
\]

Compute the factor:

\[
\ln(1+\tfrac1{300})=0.00332779\ldots
\]
\[
36\,\ln(1+\tfrac1{300})=0.11980044
\]
\[
e^{0.11980044}=1.127271813\ldots
\]

Thus  

\[
FV=5000\times1.127271813=5636.359065
\]

Rounded to the nearest cent: **\$5,636.36**.

---

**2. Flooring boxes**

Area to cover (including waste)  

\[
A_{\text{req}}=1850\times1.10=2035\ \text{sq ft}
\]

Area per box  

\[
A_{\text{box}}=23.5\ \text{sq ft}
\]

Number of boxes  

\[
N=\left\lceil\frac{2035}{23.5}\right\rceil
      =\left\lceil86.5957\ldots\right\rceil=87
\]

So **87 boxes** are needed.

---

**3. Gas cost**

Distance \(D=412\) mi, fuel economy \(E=34\) mpg, price \(P_g=\$3.89\)/gal.

Gallons needed  

\[
G=\frac{D}{E}=\frac{412}{34}=12.1176470588\ \text{gal}
\]

Cost  

\[
C=G\times P_g
  =12.1176470588\times3.89
  =46.68+0.4576470588
  =47.1376470588
\]

Rounded to the nearest cent: **\$47.14**.

---

**4. Dinner bill**

Pre‑tax amount \(A= \$172.00\).  
Total with tax \(T=\$186.40\).

Tax amount  

\[
\tau = T-A = 14.40
\]

Tip (18 % of pre‑tax)  

\[
\text{tip}=0.18\times172=30.96
\]

Total bill  

\[
B=A+\tau+\text{tip}=172+14.40+30.96=217.36
\]

Split among four people  

\[
\frac{B}{4}=54.34
\]

Each person pays **\$54.34**.
