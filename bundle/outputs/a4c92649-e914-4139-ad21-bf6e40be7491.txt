**Problem 1 – Savings account**  
The balance after \(t\) years is  

\[
A=P\Bigl(1+\frac{r}{n}\Bigr)^{nt},
\]

where \(P=5{,}000,\; r=0.04,\; n=12,\; t=3\).  
Thus  

\[
A=5{,}000\Bigl(1+\frac{0.04}{12}\Bigr)^{36}.
\]

Using the binomial expansion (or a calculator) gives  

\[
A\approx 5{,}636.36435\text{ dollars}.
\]

Rounded to the nearest cent this is **\$5 636.36**.

**Problem 2 – Flooring with waste**  
The space needs \(10\%\) extra:  

\[
\text{required area}=1850\times1.10=2035\ \text{sq ft}.
\]

Each box covers 23.5 sq ft, so the number of boxes required is  

\[
\left\lceil\frac{2035}{23.5}\right\rceil
   =\left\lceil86.0\ldots\right\rceil=87 .
\]

**Problem 3 – Gasoline cost for the road trip**  

\[
\text{gallons needed}= \frac{412\text{ miles}}{34\text{ mpg}}
                     =\frac{412}{34}=\frac{206}{17}\approx12.117647\text{ gal}.
\]

Cost (dollars)  

\[
\text{cost}=12.117647\;\text{gal}\times \$3.89/\text{gal}
           =\frac{412\times389}{34}\; \text{cents}
           =\frac{160\,268}{34}\approx4713.7647\text{ cents}.
\]

Rounding to the nearest cent gives \(4714\) cents, i.e. **\$47.14**.

**Problem 4 – Splitting the bill with a tip**  

Pre‑tax subtotal = \$172.00  
Tax already included → total before tip = \$186.40  

Tip (18 % of pre‑tax amount)  

\[
\text{tip}=0.18\times172.00=30.96\text{ dollars}.
\]

Grand total before splitting  

\[
186.40+30.96 = 217.36\text{ dollars}.
\]

Split equally among four people:  

\[
\frac{217.36}{4}=54.34\text{ dollars per person}.
\]

Thus each person pays **\$54.34** (already exact to the cent).
