**Justifications:**

1. The Equator and Tropic of Capricorn sit about 23½° apart in latitude, so only Brazil—which stretches from roughly 5°N to 33°S—is crossed by both; the more obvious Equator countries (Ecuador, Kenya) aren't touched by the southern tropic at all.

2. Iran fronts only the Persian Gulf and Yemen/Egypt only the Red Sea, but Saudi Arabia is the sole country with coastlines on both seas—its western shore faces the Red Sea and its eastern shore the Gulf.

3. Reykjavik (about 64°N) is the northernmost capital of an independent state; Nuuk in Greenland lies slightly further north but doesn't count because Greenland isn't sovereign—it's part of Denmark.

4. Russia and the United States each span 11 time zones, but France's territories scattered across every inhabited continent give it about 12, more than any other country.

5. Hawaii or California might seem close, but Florida's Key West lies across the Atlantic's narrowest stretch from Morocco/Western Africa, making Florida the closest US state to mainland Africa.

6. San Marino borders only Italy but is excluded as an Italian microstate; Lesotho—entirely enclosed by South Africa—is the only other landlocked country that touches exactly one nation.

**ANSWERS:**
1. Brazil
2. Saudi Arabia
3. Reykjavik (Iceland)
4. France
5. Florida
6. Lesotho
