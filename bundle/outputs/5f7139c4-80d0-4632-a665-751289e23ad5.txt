CITY COUNCIL APPROVES TRANSIT LEVY. The Port Ardmore City Council voted 7-2 late Monday to approve a $41.6 million transit levy spread over ten years. The money will fund three new bus routes and a rebuild of the Fulton Street station. Council member Ines Varga, who voted no, said the city had not published a cost estimate for households. The levy still needs a second reading on March 24. [S1]

Port Ardmore's council backed the transit levy on a 7-2 vote. Mayor Theo Brandt called it "the biggest investment in buses this city has made in a generation." Collection would begin July 1 if the levy passes its second reading. [S2]

WAREHOUSE FIRE ON KETTLE STREET. A fire broke out early Tuesday at a vacant warehouse on Kettle Street in Port Ardmore. Two firefighters were injured, according to an initial report from the scene. The cause is under investigation. [S3]

UPDATE, Kettle Street fire: Fire Chief Ama Mensah said one firefighter was treated for smoke inhalation and released; earlier reports that two firefighters were injured were incorrect. Crews had the fire under control by 9:15 a.m. The cause remains under investigation. [S4]

RALLY AT CITY HALL. Supporters of a proposed rent stabilization ordinance rallied outside City Hall on Monday evening. Ledger reporters at the scene estimated the crowd at about 300. [S5]

Organizers of Monday's rent stabilization rally said more than 1,000 people attended. Police estimated the crowd at 400. [S6]

TEACHER PAY BILL HEADS TO GOVERNOR. The state legislature passed a bill raising the minimum teacher salary to $48,500, by votes of 39-28 in the Senate and 81-52 in the House. Governor Lena Ruiz is expected to sign it this week, a spokesperson said. The increase would take effect in the fall term. [S7]

COLUMN: THE LEVY IS A TAX GRAB. Columnist Rob Haddad argues that the transit levy will cost a typical household $900 a year and that the council rushed the vote. "Nobody asked the people paying for it," he writes. [S9]

SCHOOL BOARD RESULTS. Delphine Okoro won the open seat on the Port Ardmore school board with 52% of the vote. [S10]
