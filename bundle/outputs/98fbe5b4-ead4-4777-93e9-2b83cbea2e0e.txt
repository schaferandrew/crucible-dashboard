The Port Ardmore City Council approved a $41.6 million transit levy in a 7-2 vote late Monday, funding three new bus routes and rebuilding Fulton Street station; Mayor Theo Brandt called it "the biggest investment in buses this city has made in a generation," while Council member Ines Varga opposed it for lack of household cost estimates, and the levy awaits a second reading on March 24. A fire at a vacant Kettle Street warehouse injured one firefighter (later corrected to smoke inhalation only) with the cause still under investigation. About 300-1,000 people rallied outside City Hall Monday evening for rent stabilization, with estimates varying by source. The state legislature passed a $48,500 minimum teacher salary bill expected to be signed this week, and Veloria’s central bank raised interest rates to 3.75% citing 4.1% inflation. An opinion column criticized the transit levy as a "tax grab" costing households $900 annually [S1-S2, S3-S4, S5-S6, S7, S8, S9 opinion].
