**Answers**

1. Brazil  
2. Saudi Arabia  
3. Reykjavik, Iceland  
4. France  
5. Alaska  
6. Lesotho  

**Justifications**

1. Brazil – The equator cuts through northern Brazil while the Tropic of Capricorn passes through its southern state of Rio Grande do Sul, making Brazil the only country crossed by both lines.  
2. Saudi Arabia – Its western coast lies on the Red Sea and its eastern coast borders the Persian Gulf, giving it shoreline on both bodies of water.  
3. Reykjavik – Located at 64°13′ N, Reykjavik is the northernmost capital of any sovereign state.  
4. France – French overseas territories span ten distinct UTC offsets, more than any other nation when mainland and territories are combined.  
5. Alaska – The easternmost point of Alaska (at 169° W) is geographically closer to Africa’s western coast than any other U.S. state, making Alaska the nearest.  
6. Lesotho – Lesotho is landlocked and shares a border only with South Africa, the sole sovereign state that borders exactly one other country.
