**Problem 1 – Savings account**

Principal \(P = \$5{,}000\)  
Nominal annual rate \(r = 4\% = 0.04\) compounded monthly → monthly rate \(\displaystyle r_m=\frac{0.04}{12}=0.003333\overline{3}\)  

Number of months \(n = 3\text{ yr}\times12 = 36\)

Future value  
\[
A = P(1+r_m)^{n}=5000\bigl(1+0.003333\overline{3}\bigr)^{36}
\]

Compute the factor:  

\[
(1.003333\overline{3})^{36}\approx 1.127267
\]

Hence  

\[
A \approx 5000\times1.127267 = \$5{,}636.335\;\text{(rounded to the cent)} = \boxed{\$5,636.34}
\]

---

**Problem 2 – Flooring**

Area needed: \(1850\text{ ft}^2\)  
Include 10 % waste → required area \(=1850\times1.10 = 2035\text{ ft}^2\)

Box size = \(23.5\text{ ft}^2\)

Number of boxes needed (rounded up)  

\[
\frac{2035}{23.5}=86.79\;\Rightarrow\; \lceil 86.79\rceil = 87
\]

So we must buy **87 boxes**.

---

**Problem 3 – Gas cost**

Miles driven \(=412\)  
Fuel efficiency \(=34\text{ mpg}\) → gallons used  

\[
g = \frac{412}{34}=12.117647\text{ gal}
\]

Gas price \(= \$3.89/\text{gal}\)

Cost  

\[
C = 12.117647\times3.89 \approx \$47.1376\;\text{(rounded to the cent)} = \boxed{\$47.14}
\]

---

**Problem 4 – Dinner split**

Pre‑tax amount \(= \$172.00\)  
Tax included makes total \(= \$186.40\) (tax = \$14.40)  

Tip 18 % on pre‑tax:  

\[
\text{Tip}=0.18\times172 = \$30.96
\]

Grand total (price + tax + tip)  

\[
T = 186.40 + 30.96 = \$217.36
\]

Split four ways:  

\[
\frac{217.36}{4}= \$54.34
\]

Each person pays **\$54.34**.

---

**ANSWERS**

1. $5,636.34  
2. 87 boxes  
3. $47.14  
4. $54.34
