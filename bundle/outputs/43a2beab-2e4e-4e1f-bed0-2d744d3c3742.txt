I favored names that feel plain, slightly formal, or Midwestern rather than folksy: Polish-American heritage can be present in the surname without turning the character into a caricature. The strongest options balance reserve with intelligence and dryness—names that could belong to someone who notices things precisely but rarely performs warmth.
