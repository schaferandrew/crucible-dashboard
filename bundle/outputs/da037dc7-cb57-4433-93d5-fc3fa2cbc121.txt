The events can be dated as follows:

- **Magna Carta** was signed in 1215, making it the earliest event.
- The **Gutenberg Bible** was printed around 1455, after Magna Carta but before the modern era.
- The **French Revolution** began in 1789 with the storming of the Bastille, well after the printing press.
- **World War I** started in 1914, following the Revolution by over a century.
- The **first Moon landing** occurred in 1969, during the Cold War era.
- Finally, the **fall of the Berlin Wall** happened in 1989, marking a significant moment in late‑20th‑century history.

ORDER:
1. The signing of Magna Carta — 1215
2. The printing of the Gutenberg Bible — 1455
3. The start of the French Revolution — 1789
4. The start of the First World War — 1914
5. The first Moon landing — 1969
6. The fall of the Berlin Wall — 1989
