### 1. Savings Account Calculation
To calculate the future value of the savings account, we use the compound interest formula:

\[ A = P \left(1 + \frac{r}{n}\right)^{nt} \]

Where:
- \( A \) is the amount of money accumulated after n years, including interest.
- \( P \) is the principal amount (the initial amount of money).
- \( r \) is the annual interest rate (decimal).
- \( n \) is the number of times that interest is compounded per year.
- \( t \) is the time the money is invested for in years.

Given:
- \( P = 5000 \)
- \( r = 0.04 \) (4% annual interest rate)
- \( n = 12 \) (compounded monthly)
- \( t = 3 \) years

Plugging in the values:

\[ A = 5000 \left(1 + \frac{0.04}{12}\right)^{12 \times 3} \]

First, calculate the monthly interest rate:

\[ \frac{0.04}{12} = 0.003333... \]

Next, add 1 to this value:

\[ 1 + 0.003333... = 1.003333... \]

Now, raise this to the power of \( nt \):

\[ (1.003333...)^{36} \]

Calculating this:

\[ (1.003333...)^{36} \approx 1.127485 \]

Finally, multiply by the principal:

\[ A = 5000 \times 1.127485 \approx 5637.425 \]

Rounding to the nearest cent:

\[ A \approx 5637.43 \]

### 2. Flooring Calculation
First, calculate the total area needed including waste:

\[ \text{Total Area} = \text{Floor Area} + (\text{Waste Percentage} \times \text{Floor Area}) \]
\[ \text{Total Area} = 1850 + (0.10 \times 1850) \]
\[ \text{Total Area} = 1850 + 185 \]
\[ \text{Total Area} = 2035 \text{ sq ft} \]

Next, determine the number of boxes needed:

\[ \text{Number of Boxes} = \frac{\text{Total Area}}{\text{Area per Box}} \]
\[ \text{Number of Boxes} = \frac{2035}{23.5} \]
\[ \text{Number of Boxes} \approx 86.5957 \]

Since you can't buy a fraction of a box, round up to the next whole number:

\[ \text{Number of Boxes} = 87 \]

### 3. Gas Cost Calculation
First, calculate the total amount of gas needed for the trip:

\[ \text{Gas Needed} = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Miles per Gallon}} \]
\[ \text{Gas Needed} = \frac{412}{34} \]
\[ \text{Gas Needed} \approx 12.1176 \text{ gallons} \]

Next, calculate the total cost of gas:

\[ \text{Total Cost} = \text{Gas Needed} \times \text{Price per Gallon} \]
\[ \text{Total Cost} = 12.1176 \times 3.89 \]
\[ \text{Total Cost} \approx 47.1506 \]

Rounding to the nearest cent:

\[ \text{Total Cost} \approx 47.15 \]

### 4. Dinner Bill Calculation
First, calculate the tip amount:

\[ \text{Tip} = \text{Pre-Tax Amount} \times \text{Tip Percentage} \]
\[ \text{Tip} = 172.00 \times 0.18 \]
\[ \text{Tip} = 30.96 \]

Next, calculate the total bill including tip:

\[ \text{Total Bill} = \text{Dinner Cost} + \text{Tip} \]
\[ \text{Total Bill} = 186.40 + 30.96 \]
\[ \text{Total Bill} = 217.36 \]

Finally, split the total bill among four people:

\[ \text{Each Person Pays} = \frac{\text{Total Bill}}{4} \]
\[ \text{Each Person Pays} = \frac{217.36}{4} \]
\[ \text{Each Person Pays} = 54.34 \]
