## 1. Savings account (compound interest)

**Formula:** A = P(1 + r/n)^(nt)

- P = $5,000, r = 0.04, n = 12, t = 3

$$A = 5000\left(1 + \frac{0.04}{12}\right)^{12\times3} = 5000\,(1.003333...)^{36}$$

Monthly rate: 0.04 ÷ 12 = 0.003333…
Growth factor: (1.003333…)³⁶ ≈ 1.12727190

$$A = 5000 \times 1.12727190 = 5636.359...$$

**≈ $5,636.36**

## 2. Flooring boxes

Total area needed (with waste):
$$1850 \times 1.10 = 2035\text{ sq ft}$$

Boxes required:
$$\frac{2035}{23.5} = 86.5957...$$

You can't buy a partial box, so round **up**.

**= 87 boxes** (check: 87 × 23.5 = 2044.5 sq ft ≥ 2035 ✓)

## 3. Road trip gas cost

Gallons needed:
$$\frac{412}{34} = 12.117647... \text{ gallons}$$

Cost (don't round the gallons yet):
$$12.117647... \times 3.89 = 47.1376...$$

**≈ $47.14**

---

## 4. Dinner split four ways

Tip is on the pre-tax amount:
$$0.18 \times 172.00 = 30.96$$

Total to split (bill including tax + tip):
$$186.40 + 30.96 = 217.36$$

Per person:
$$\frac{217.36}{4} = 54.34$$

**≈ $54.34 each**

*(Note: if "the total" is meant to be pre-tax + tip only, excluding the tax already baked into the bill, it would be (172.00 + 30.96) ÷ 4 = $50.74.)*
