2. Saudi Arabia has a Red Sea coastline on its western side and a Persian Gulf coastline on its eastern side.  
3. Reykjavik is the northernmost capital of a sovereign state because farther-north settlements such as Nuuk, Longyearbyen, and Iqaluit are not capitals of sovereign states.  
4. Counting overseas territories, France spans more time zones than any other country because its possessions extend from French Polynesia at UTC minus 10 to Wallis and Futuna at UTC plus 12.  
5. Florida is nearest to the Cape Verde Islands, but for mainland Africa the shortest great-circle distance from any United States state runs from eastern Maine toward Morocco or Western Sahara.  
6. Lesotho is completely surrounded by South Africa, so it has no coastline and exactly one neighboring country.
