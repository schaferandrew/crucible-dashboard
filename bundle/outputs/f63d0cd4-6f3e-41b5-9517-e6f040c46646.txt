**1. Compound Interest Calculation**
Principal \(P = \$5{,}000\), annual rate \(r = 4\% = 0.04\), compounded monthly (\(n = 12\)), time \(t = 3\) years.
Formula: \(A = P\left(1 + \frac{r}{n}\right)^{nt}\)
\[
A = 5000\left(1 + \frac{0.04}{12}\right)^{12 \times 3}
= 5000\left(1 + 0.003333\ldots\right)^{36}
= 5000(1.003333\ldots)^{36}
\]
The growth factor \((1.003333\ldots)^{36} \approx 1.1272718\).  
\(A \approx 5000 \times 1.1272718 = 5636.359\)  
Rounded to the cent: **$5,636.36**

**2. Flooring Boxes Needed**
Area = \(1{,}850\ \text{sq ft}\). Waste factor = 10%, so total material needed:
\[
1850 \times 1.10 = 2035\ \text{sq ft}
\]
Each box covers \(23.5\ \text{sq ft}\).
Number of boxes required:
\[
\frac{2035}{23.5} = 86.5957\ldots
\]
Since boxes are sold whole and you must cover the full area (including waste), round up to the next whole box: **87 boxes**.

**3. Gas Cost for Road Trip**
Distance = \(412\) miles, fuel efficiency = \(34\) mpg.
Gallons needed:
\[
\frac{412}{34} = 12.117647\ldots \text{gallons}
\]
Gas price = \(\$3.89\)/gallon.
Total cost:
\[
\left(\frac{412}{34}\right) \times 3.89 = \frac{412 \times 3.89}{34} = \frac{1602.68}{34} = 47.137647\ldots
\]
Rounded to the cent: **$47.14**

**4. Splitting Dinner Bill**
Total including tax = \(\$186.40\), pre-tax amount = \(\$172.00\).  
Tip = \(18\%\) of pre-tax:
\[
0.18 \times 172.00 = \$30.96
\]
Total amount to split:
\[
186.40 + 30.96 = \$217.36
\]
Split four ways:
\[
\frac{217.36}{4} = \$54.34
\]
Each person pays **$54.34**.
