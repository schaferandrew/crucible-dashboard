1. Brazil – Brazil is the only country through which both the Equator and the Tropic of Capricorn pass, with the Equator crossing its northern Amapá region and the Tropic its southern interior.  
2. Saudi Arabia – Saudi Arabia is the sole nation possessing coastlines on both the Red Sea and the Persian Gulf, as no other country borders both water bodies.  
3. Reykjavík – Despite Moscow being a northern capital, Reykjavík, Iceland at approximately 64°N is the northernmost capital city of any sovereign state, outranking Helsinki and others.  
4. France – Counting all overseas territories, France spans 12 time zones, more than any other country, despite its mainland using just one.  
5. Maine – Despite Florida's southern tip being commonly assumed, Maine’s easternmost point is the closest US mainland location to Africa, with a great-circle distance of roughly 3,150 miles to Tunisia.  
6. Lesotho – Lesotho is the only landlocked sovereign country that borders exactly one other nation (South Africa), aside from the excluded Italian microstates.
