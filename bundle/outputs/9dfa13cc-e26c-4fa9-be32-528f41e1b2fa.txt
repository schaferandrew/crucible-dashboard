1. Brazil is the only country whose territory extends from north of the Equator to south of the Tropic of Capricorn.
2. Saudi Arabia borders both the Red Sea on its west and the Persian Gulf on its east.
3. Reykjavík, Iceland’s capital, sits farther north than any other sovereign state’s capital (excluding non-sovereign Greenland).
4. France’s mainland plus overseas territories spread across more distinct time zones than any other country when those territories are counted.
5. North Carolina’s Outer Banks/Cape Hatteras area is the closest part of the US to mainland Africa, especially western Sahara/Mauritania across the Atlantic.
6. Lesotho is fully enclosed by South Africa and therefore has only one land border.

ANSWERS
1. Brazil
2. Saudi Arabia
3. Reykjavík
4. France
5. North Carolina
6. Lesotho
