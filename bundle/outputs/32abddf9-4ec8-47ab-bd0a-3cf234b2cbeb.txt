2. Powers of 7 mod 100 cycle as 7, 49, 43, 1. Since \(2026\equiv 2\pmod 4\), remainder is 49.

3. Let \(T_n\) be the number of tilings. First column: vertical domino gives \(T_{n-1}\); or a square/two horizontal dominoes across columns 1–2 gives \(2T_{n-2}\). With \(T_0=1,T_1=1\), this yields \(T_{12}=2731\).

4. \(P(\max=4)=P(all\le4)-P(all\le3)=(4/6)^3-(3/6)^3=37/216\).

5. Let \(E\) be the event that at least one child is a boy born Tuesday.  
   \(P(E)=1-(13/14)^2=27/196\). Both boys and \(E\): \((1/4)[1-(6/7)^2]=13/196\). Ratio = \(13/27\).
