**Key implementation details:**
- Iterates month-by-month to correctly handle contributions at calendar month ends regardless of compounding frequency
- For `--months N`, converts to total months and determines which compounding periods fall within that time
- Daily compounding maps periods to actual calendar days using cumulative month-day counts
- All validation exits with non-zero status and a short message on stderr (no tracebacks)
