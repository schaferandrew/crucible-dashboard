1. Which country is crossed by both the Equator and the Tropic of Capricorn?
   - Kenya. The Equator crosses northern Kenya, and the Tropic of Capricorn crosses southern Kenya.

2. Which country has coastline on both the Red Sea and the Persian Gulf?
   - Yemen. Yemen has a coastline on both the Red Sea and the Persian Gulf.

3. What is the northernmost capital city of a sovereign state?
   - Reykjavík, Iceland. Reykjavík is the northernmost capital city of a sovereign state.

4. Counting overseas territories, which country spans the most time zones?
   - Russia. Russia spans more time zones than any other country, including its overseas territories.

5. Which US state is closest to mainland Africa?
   - Florida. Florida is the US state closest to mainland Africa.

6. Apart from the microstates inside Italy, which is the only landlocked country that borders exactly one other country?
   - San Marino. San Marino is the only landlocked country that borders exactly one other country, which is Italy.

ANSWERS
1. Kenya
2. Yemen
3. Reykjavík, Iceland
4. Russia
5. Florida
6. San Marino
